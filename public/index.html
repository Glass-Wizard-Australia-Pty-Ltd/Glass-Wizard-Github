🎬 Glass Wizard Video Blueprint (2:41)

A direct CapCut edit timeline mapped to your track. Build in order and cut on the beat.

0:00–0:07 · Intro Atmosphere

  • Dark screen fade-in to neon city / stage with slow zoom.
  • Add low fog + particles + subtle glow.
  • Text: Glass Wizard Australia Pty Ltd.
  • CapCut: fade-in ~1.5s + camera movement slow zoom.

0:07–0:20 · Intro Vocals / First Energy

  • Avatar 1 enters as main artist.
  • Cut every 2–3 seconds with slight zoom + head nod animation.
  • Low RGB split + subtle beat-sync shake.
  • Cut directly on vocal hits.

0:20–0:40 · Flow Starts

  • Alternate Avatar 1 and Avatar 2 every 2 seconds.
  • Shift left/right positioning for stage presence.
  • Use flash transitions on beat accents.

0:40–1:00 · First Lift / Hook Build

  • Bring in all avatars via split screen or fast intercuts.
  • On each beat: micro zoom + slight 1–2° rotation.
  • Increase glow and add light leaks.

1:00–1:20 · Hook / Chorus Peak 🔥

  • Use all avatars with 1–2 second cut speed.
  • Add medium camera shake + stronger RGB split + flash cuts.
  • Use the brightest neon background variation.

1:20–1:40 · Drop Back / Reset

  • Return focus to Avatar 1.
  • Slow zoom, minimal shake, heavier fog, lower brightness.
  • Keep contrast so next drop feels bigger.

1:40–2:05 · Second Build

  • Bring Avatar 2 + 3 back with faster switching.
  • Add bounce animations and beat flashes.
  • Build glow intensity again.

2:05–2:25 · Final Peak 🔥

  • Fast cuts at 1 second or faster.
  • Layer avatars as overlays + quick zoom punches.
  • Use motion blur, flash transitions, and heavy glow.

2:25–2:41 · Outro / Fade Out

  • Slow down to one avatar then fade to black.
  • Text: [Project Title] · [Optional Tagline].
  • Then: [Optional credits or acknowledgements].
  • CapCut: 2–3s fade-out + gradual audio reduction.

⚡ Quick Build Checklist

  1. Import audio in CapCut.
  2. Drop beat markers while playing (M).
  3. Follow each section above in order.
  4. Cut clips to markers.
  5. Add zoom keyframes + shake curve (low → high → low) + glow/RGB.

Pro timing rule: Kick = quick zoom, Snare = shake, Vocal = cut. Only animate when sound moves.
